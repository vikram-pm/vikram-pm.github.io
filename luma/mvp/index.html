Luma · Mobile MVP

Public demo. This version runs the example flow with a built-in rules engine, so it works without signing in. Tap a “Try saying” suggestion on the phone. Read the case study

The first end-to-end slice of Luma. Say a family goal out loud; Luma spots it, proposes a plan, adds the sessions as tasks once you agree, then tracks progress and follows up. Your goals and tasks are saved in this browser, so they’re here next time you open it.
